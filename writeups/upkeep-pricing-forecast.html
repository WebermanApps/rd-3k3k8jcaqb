2. Tier take-rate evidence

  • Simonson & Tversky camera study: adding a top-priced third option moved the middle option to 57%, cheapest 22%, top 21% [S] https://www.ux-strategy.ch/en/effects/false-dichotomy.html , https://wepushers.com/frameworks/good-better-best-tiering/
  • Simonson (1989): an option gained an average 17.5 points of share when it became the compromise option, across 5 categories [S] https://a.drupalgsb-test.paas.cc.columbia.edu/sites/default/files-efs/pubfiles/758/Alternative_Models_for_Capturing_the_Compromise_Effect.pdf , https://www.buynomics.com/glossary-blog/middleoption
  • A practitioner rule of thumb (the author says it is not published research): if 70%+ land on Good, Better is too weak [S] https://mmsvegas.com/library/good-better-best-pricing-explained/
  • I found no public data on tier mix for home-maintenance memberships specifically. Comparable menus: Monty's $19/$49/$109/$150 per month https://www.getmontys.com/membership ; Upkeepers from $59/mo https://getupkeepers.com/ [S]
  • HVAC agreements: retention 70–85% (Service Roundtable/NCI/ACCA band) https://thefenneclab.com/hvac-operations/hvac-maintenance-agreement-ltv-calculator/ ; top companies renew 78–85%, the industry average is 55–65%, and renewal falls to 40–55% when the only reminder is an email https://www.builtontenth.com/hvac-research/how-hvac-service-agreements-add-200k-annual-revenue ; 15–20% of a contractor's customer base on agreements is "functional", 25–35% is "strong" (same source); 86% renewal KPI (Contracting Business, via) https://www.webtonic.io/blog/hvac-lifecycle-and-retention-marketing-statistics ; MCAA 2025: PM retention 93%+ (commercial-leaning) https://dev.mcaa.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/2025-Benchmark-Survey-Final.pdf

Answer (1): Plus is the most likely pick. [A] It is in the middle, it carries the "recommended" flag, and gutters and irrigation are visible Michigan pain points. One caveat: Complete costs 1.9x Plus, which is a wide gap, so it will pull fewer buyers than the 21% in the camera study. Essentials at $99 will draw price-sensitive and small-home buyers.

Caveats [A]: - Tier figures use "From" prices. Real quotes for typical 2–3k sq ft Farmington Hills homes will be higher, so the base case is likely understated on tiers. - The 2-year welcome offer ("pay 24, get 30 months") cuts revenue per month of service by 20% for anyone who takes it. On cash collected during the paid term it does not reduce spend. - Lawn and snow are seasonal cash. Annualizing smooths them out, but actual cash is lumpy: about $40×4.3 ≈ $173/mo extra Apr–Oct for lawn customers, and snow is usually prepaid in the fall. - For retention, plan on 70–85% annual renewal if you call members before renewal, and 40–55% if you only send an email (sources above). - Every attach rate here is an assumption. No public attach data exists for home-maintenance memberships. Replace these with beta data (the add-on event logging is already in the app).
